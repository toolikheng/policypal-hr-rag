## 1. Purpose and Application

1.1 This Code sets out the standards of behaviour expected of everyone who works for Binaan Maju Sdn Bhd ("the Company"), including permanent, contract and probationary employees, interns and secondees.

1.2 A breach of this Code may lead to disciplinary action under section 9. Where this Code and another Company policy differ, the stricter rule applies.

## 2. Core Values

The Company's four core values are:

- **Integrity**: we do what is right, even when no one is watching.
- **Safety**: everyone goes home safe, every day.
- **Respect**: we treat colleagues, clients and communities with dignity.
- **Excellence**: we deliver quality work on time.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

3.1 Employees must avoid situations where their personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of the Company.

3.2 Employees must declare in writing to HR:

(a) any business they own or work for outside the Company;

(b) any family member who works for, or owns more than 5% of, a supplier, subcontractor, client or competitor;

(c) any other situation that could affect their judgement.

3.3 Declarations are made on joining, every January in the annual declaration, and whenever circumstances change.

3.4 Outside employment or business activities need prior written approval from the HOD and HR, and must not use Company time or resources.

## 4. Gifts, Hospitality and Anti-Bribery

### 4.1 Receiving Gifts

4.1.1 Employees may accept small gifts worth up to RM100, such as festive food hampers or branded souvenirs, provided the giver is not in a tender or negotiation with the Company at the time.

4.1.2 Gifts worth more than RM100 must be declared to the Compliance Unit within three working days and either returned or surrendered to the Company.

4.1.3 Cash, cash vouchers, gift cards and loans must never be accepted, whatever the amount.

> **Amendment (version 2.1, 1 September 2024):** The gift limit was reduced from RM200 to RM100.

### 4.2 Giving Gifts and Hospitality

4.2.1 Gifts and hospitality offered to clients or business partners must be modest, infrequent and recorded. Anything above RM300 per person needs prior approval from the Compliance Unit.

4.2.2 Any gift or hospitality for a government official, or for a person working for a government-linked client, needs prior approval from the Compliance Unit, whatever its value.

4.2.3 No gift or hospitality may be offered to anyone involved in a live tender evaluation.

### 4.3 Bribery and Facilitation Payments

The Company has zero tolerance for bribery. Employees must not offer, give, request or accept any payment or advantage to improperly influence a decision. Facilitation payments ("duit kopi") to speed up permits, inspections or approvals are prohibited, even if small or customary.

## 5. Workplace Behaviour

### 5.1 Dress Code

5.1.1 Office-based employees wear smart casual attire from Monday to Thursday. On Fridays, employees may wear batik or the Company polo shirt.

5.1.2 At project sites, personal protective equipment (PPE) is mandatory in all work areas, whatever the dress code: a safety helmet, safety boots and a high-visibility vest, plus any additional PPE the task requires.

5.1.3 Employees meeting clients should wear business attire.

### 5.2 Anti-Harassment

5.2.1 The Company does not tolerate harassment of any kind, whether by employees, managers, clients, subcontractors or visitors, and whether it happens at the office, at project sites, at Company events or online.

5.2.2 Harassment means unwelcome behaviour that a reasonable person would find offensive, humiliating or intimidating. It includes sexual harassment, such as unwelcome physical contact, comments about a person's body, sexual jokes or images, and requests for sexual favours. It also includes bullying, such as repeated shouting, public humiliation, deliberately excluding someone from work activities, and threats. Harassment based on race, religion, gender, age, disability or nationality is treated with particular seriousness, given the diversity of the Company's workforce across offices and sites.

5.2.3 An employee who experiences or witnesses harassment may report it to any of the following: their immediate superior, any HR officer, the Head of Human Resources, or the whistleblowing channel in section 8. The employee does not need to raise the matter with the alleged harasser first. Reports may be made verbally or in writing. HR records verbal reports in writing and asks the employee to confirm the record.

5.2.4 HR acknowledges every report within two working days and appoints an investigating officer who has no reporting line to either party. Investigations are normally completed within 14 working days. Where needed, the Company may temporarily change work arrangements while the investigation is carried out, for example by moving one party to a different team or site. Such interim measures are not a finding of guilt.

5.2.5 Both parties may give their account, name witnesses and provide evidence. The outcome is given to both parties in writing. Where a complaint is upheld, disciplinary action is taken under section 9.

5.2.6 Retaliation against anyone who reports harassment in good faith, or who helps an investigation, is itself serious misconduct. Knowingly making a false complaint is also misconduct.

### 5.3 Attendance and Punctuality

5.3.1 Employees record attendance with the MyHR mobile app at their office or site location.

5.3.2 Arriving more than 15 minutes late without a valid reason counts as late attendance. Three late attendances in one calendar month lead to a verbal warning, and repeated lateness after that leads to a written warning.

## 6. Safety at Project Sites

6.1 Every employee and visitor must follow the site safety rules and the instructions of the site safety officer.

6.2 Alcohol and illegal drugs are strictly prohibited on all Company premises and sites. Employees at sites may be tested for alcohol or drugs without notice.

6.3 Every employee has stop-work authority. Anyone may stop work they believe is unsafe, without fear of blame, and must report it to the site supervisor immediately.

6.4 All accidents, injuries, near misses and dangerous occurrences must be reported to the site safety officer within 24 hours.

## 7. Company Assets and Information

### 7.1 IT Equipment and Email

Company laptops, phones and email are for business use. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with work. Employees must not install unlicensed software, share passwords, or connect unknown devices to the Company network.

### 7.2 Social Media

7.2.1 Employees must not post photos or videos of project sites, drawings or documents without approval from Corporate Communications.

7.2.2 Only authorised spokespersons may speak on behalf of the Company, including online. Employees who mention their employer online must make clear that their views are their own.

### 7.3 Confidential Information

Tender documents, pricing, client information, drawings and employees' personal data are confidential. They may only be shared with people who need them for their work, and must not be taken or sent outside the Company without authorisation. This duty continues after employment ends.

## 8. Whistleblowing

8.1 Employees and members of the public may report suspected misconduct, fraud, bribery or safety concerns to the Compliance Unit through the ethics channel at ethics@binaanmaju.example, or in a sealed letter marked "Confidential: Compliance Unit".

8.2 Reports may be anonymous. The identity of a whistleblower who gives their name is kept confidential and shared only with the investigation team.

8.3 Whistleblowers acting in good faith are protected from dismissal, demotion and any other retaliation, even if their concern is not proven.

## 9. Disciplinary Action

9.1 Depending on how serious the breach is, the Company may take one or more of the following actions: verbal warning, written warning, final written warning, suspension without pay for up to 14 days, demotion, or dismissal.

9.2 Serious misconduct, such as theft, fraud, bribery, false claims, violence, harassment or working under the influence of alcohol or drugs, may lead to dismissal after a domestic inquiry.

9.3 While serious misconduct is being investigated, the employee may be suspended on half pay for up to two weeks.

9.4 Employees may appeal against a disciplinary decision in writing to the Head of Human Resources within seven days.
