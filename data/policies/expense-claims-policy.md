# Expense Claims Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This policy covers business expenses that employees of Binaan Maju Sdn Bhd ("the Company") incur at their normal work location and on local trips of up to 100 km from their base office or site.

1.2 Expenses for outstation travel (more than 100 km from the base office or site) and for overseas travel are covered by the Travel Policy (BM-HR-POL-005), not by this policy.

1.3 Medical expenses are covered by the Medical Claims and Benefits Policy (BM-HR-POL-002).

## 2. General Principles

2.1 Expenses must be necessary for Company business, reasonable in amount, and supported by original receipts.

2.2 All claims are submitted through the eClaim system and approved according to section 9.

2.3 A receipt is required for every item above RM20. Items of RM20 or less without a receipt may be claimed with a written explanation, up to RM100 per month.

2.4 Employees must not split one expense into several claims to stay below an approval limit.

## 3. Local Transport

### 3.1 Mileage for Using Your Own Vehicle

3.1.1 Employees who use their own vehicle for Company business are paid mileage:

| Vehicle | First 500 km in a calendar month | Above 500 km in the same month |
|---|---|---|
| Car | RM0.70 per km | RM0.55 per km |
| Motorcycle | RM0.35 per km | RM0.35 per km |

3.1.2 Travel between home and the employee's usual workplace is not business travel and cannot be claimed. Where an employee goes directly from home to another work location, only the distance in excess of the normal home-to-workplace trip may be claimed.

3.1.3 Mileage claims must state the date, the start and end points, the purpose and the distance. Distances are checked against an online map.

> **Amendment (version 4.0, 1 January 2025):** The car rate for the first 500 km was increased from RM0.60 to RM0.70 per km. The rate above 500 km is unchanged.

### 3.2 Tolls and Parking

Tolls and parking for business trips are reimbursed at cost. Attach the receipt, or the toll card statement showing the transaction. Parking at the employee's own usual workplace is not reimbursed.

### 3.3 Taxi and E-hailing

Taxi and e-hailing fares are reimbursed for business trips when using an own vehicle is not practical, and for the journey home after approved overtime that ends after 11:00 p.m. (see the Overtime Policy, section 7.2). Employees should choose the standard, not the premium, service type.

## 4. Meals and Entertainment

### 4.1 Client Entertainment

4.1.1 Entertainment of clients, consultants and business partners must have a clear business purpose. The claim must list the guests, their organisations and the purpose.

4.1.2 Limits per occasion:

| Grade | Limit per occasion |
|---|---|
| NE1 to NE4 | Prior approval by the HOD required |
| E1 to E3 | RM150 |
| M1 and M2 | RM300 |
| SM | RM600 |

4.1.3 Alcohol is not reimbursable. Entertainment of government officials must also follow the Code of Conduct (BM-HR-POL-006), section 4.2.

### 4.2 Working Meals

When a team must work through a meal to meet an approved project deadline, the most senior employee present may claim up to RM25 per person. This does not apply to overtime meals, which are paid under the Overtime Policy, section 7.1, or to meals during outstation travel, which are covered by the Travel Policy.

## 5. Mobile Phone and Internet

5.1 Employees whose roles require them to be reachable outside office hours receive a monthly reimbursement of their personal mobile phone bill:

| Grade or role | Monthly limit |
|---|---|
| E1 to E3 in site or field roles | RM80 |
| M1 and M2 | RM150 |
| SM | RM250 |

5.2 Home internet bills are not claimed under this policy. Employees on an approved work-from-home arrangement receive a fixed allowance under the Work From Home Policy (BM-HR-POL-008), section 5.2.

## 6. Professional Memberships

Annual membership fees for one professional body directly relevant to the employee's role, for example an engineering or quantity surveying institution, are reimbursed up to RM500 per year with HOD approval. Fees for a second membership need Director approval.

## 7. Items That Cannot Be Claimed

(a) traffic summonses, parking fines and other penalties;

(b) alcohol;

(c) personal items such as clothing, grooming products and toiletries;

(d) late payment charges on personal bills;

(e) expenses already paid by another party, or already claimed under another policy;

(f) donations or gifts, except as approved under the Code of Conduct, section 4.2.

## 8. Submission Deadlines

8.1 Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. The monthly cut-off is the 25th, and claims submitted by the cut-off are paid with that month's salary.

8.2 Claims submitted 31 to 90 days after the expense need HOD approval with a reason for the delay. Claims older than 90 days are not paid.

## 9. Approval Limits

| Claim amount | Approved by |
|---|---|
| Up to RM500 | Immediate superior |
| RM500.01 to RM2,000 | Head of Department |
| Above RM2,000 | Finance Director |

Claims made by an HOD are approved by the HOD's own superior. No one may approve their own claim.
