## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the medical, dental and optical benefits provided by Binaan Maju Sdn Bhd ("the Company") and how to claim them. It covers the cost of treatment. Time off work because of illness is covered by the Leave Policy (BM-HR-POL-001), section 5.

## 2. Eligibility and Covered Family Members

2.1 Confirmed employees are covered together with their legal spouse and up to five (5) unmarried children under 21 years of age, or under 24 years of age if in full-time tertiary education.

2.2 Employees on probation are covered for themselves only, for outpatient treatment at panel clinics. Group hospitalisation insurance (section 4.2) covers probationers from their first day of service. Cover for family members starts on the date of confirmation.

2.3 Where both spouses work for the Company, each child may be registered under one parent only.

2.4 Family members must be registered in MyHR before any claim can be made for them.

## 3. Outpatient Treatment

### 3.1 Panel Clinics

The Company has panel clinics near every office and major project site. At a panel clinic, the employee shows their staff ID card and pays nothing for the consultation and prescribed medicine, up to the annual outpatient limit in section 3.2. The current panel clinic list is on MyHR under "Benefits".

### 3.2 Annual Outpatient Limits

| Grade | Annual outpatient limit (employee and family combined) |
|---|---|
| NE1 to NE4 | RM800 |
| E1 to E3 | RM1,200 |
| M1 and M2 | RM1,800 |
| SM | RM2,500 |

The limit applies per calendar year and covers panel clinic visits, approved non-panel claims (section 3.3) and specialist consultations (section 4.1). Unused limits do not carry forward.

> **Amendment 2 (version 2.2, 1 July 2025):** The limit for E1 to E3 was increased from RM1,000 to RM1,200. Claims for treatment received before 1 July 2025 use the old limit.

### 3.3 Non-Panel Clinics and Reimbursement

3.3.1 Treatment at a non-panel clinic is reimbursed only if no panel clinic was reasonably available, for example outside clinic hours, while travelling outstation, or in an emergency.

3.3.2 Reimbursement is capped at RM80 per visit and counts toward the annual outpatient limit.

3.3.3 Submit the claim in the eClaim system within 30 days of the visit, with the itemised receipt. Claims without an itemised receipt are rejected.

### 3.4 Medical Certificates

A medical certificate (MC) is not needed to claim treatment costs. MCs are needed only to cover absence from work. See the Leave Policy, section 5.1.

## 4. Specialist and Hospital Treatment

### 4.1 Specialist Consultation

Specialist consultations are covered only with a referral letter from a panel clinic doctor. They count toward the annual outpatient limit in section 3.2. Follow-up visits within three months of the referral do not need a new referral letter.

### 4.2 Group Hospitalisation and Surgical Insurance

All employees, including those on probation, are covered by the Company's group hospitalisation and surgical insurance from their first day of service.

| Grade | Room and board per day | Overall annual limit |
|---|---|---|
| NE1 to NE4 | RM150 | RM50,000 |
| E1 to E3 | RM200 | RM50,000 |
| M1 and M2 | RM300 | RM80,000 |
| SM | RM400 | RM80,000 |

The registered spouse and children of a confirmed employee are covered at the same room-and-board rate as the employee. If the employee chooses a room above their entitlement, they pay the difference.

### 4.3 Hospital Admission Procedure

4.3.1 For a planned admission, request a guarantee letter (GL) from the insurer through HR at least three working days before admission.

4.3.2 For an emergency admission, show the insurance card at the hospital and inform HR within 24 hours, or on the next working day.

4.3.3 The employee must also apply for hospitalisation leave under the Leave Policy, section 5.2.

## 5. Dental Benefits

Confirmed employees may claim up to RM300 per calendar year for dental treatment for themselves only: consultation, scaling and polishing, fillings and extractions. Braces, whitening, veneers and other cosmetic treatment are not covered. Dental claims are separate from the outpatient limit.

## 6. Optical Benefits

Confirmed employees may claim up to RM400 once every two calendar years for prescription spectacles or contact lenses for themselves only. An eye test report from an optometrist is required. Sunglasses are not covered. Prescription safety eyewear needed for site work is provided by the Company and is not claimed under this section.

## 7. Exclusions

The following are not covered under any part of this policy:

(a) cosmetic or aesthetic treatment;

(b) fertility treatment;

(c) vitamins, supplements and health products that are not prescribed for a diagnosed condition;

(d) traditional and complementary medicine, except physiotherapy by a registered physiotherapist with a doctor's referral, up to RM500 per year within the outpatient limit;

(e) treatment for injuries caused by breaking the law, or by being under the influence of alcohol or drugs;

(f) pre-existing conditions during the first 12 months of hospitalisation insurance cover, where the insurer excludes them.

## 8. Claim Submission and Deadlines

8.1 All claims are submitted in eClaim with original, itemised receipts.

8.2 Claims must be submitted within 30 days of treatment. Claims submitted 31 to 60 days after treatment need HOD approval with a reason. Claims older than 60 days are not paid.

8.3 Approved claims submitted by the 20th of the month are paid with that month's salary.

8.4 Making a false claim is serious misconduct under the Code of Conduct (BM-HR-POL-006), section 9.
