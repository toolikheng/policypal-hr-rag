## 1. Purpose

This policy explains the probation period for new employees of Binaan Maju Sdn Bhd ("the Company"), how performance is reviewed during probation, and how employees are confirmed in their appointment.

## 2. Probation Period

2.1 New permanent employees serve a probation period starting on their first day of work:

| Grade | Probation period |
|---|---|
| NE1 to NE4 | 3 months |
| E1 to E3 | 6 months |
| M1, M2 and SM | 6 months |

2.2 Employees who transfer between Company offices or sites do not serve a new probation period.

2.3 Employees promoted from a Non-Executive grade to an Executive grade serve a three-month performance review period in the new role, but are not on probation for employment purposes.

## 3. Reviews During Probation

3.1 The immediate superior holds a mid-probation review halfway through the probation period, using the Probation Review Form in MyHR, and agrees improvement actions where needed.

3.2 A final review is held at least two weeks before the probation period ends. The superior recommends one of: confirmation, extension of probation, or termination.

3.3 Reviews assess the quality and quantity of work, attendance and punctuality, safety behaviour (for site-based employees), teamwork, and conduct in line with the Code of Conduct.

## 4. Extension of Probation

4.1 Probation may be extended once, by up to three months, if the employee's performance or conduct has not yet met the required standard.

4.2 The employee must be told about the extension in writing before the original probation period ends, with the reasons and the improvement expected.

4.3 Probation cannot be extended a second time. At the end of an extension, the employee is either confirmed or their employment is ended under section 8.

## 5. Confirmation

5.1 HR issues a confirmation letter within 14 days after a successful final review. Confirmation takes effect from the day after the probation period ends.

5.2 An employee whose probation period has ended, and who has not received either a confirmation letter or an extension letter, should contact HR.

5.3 On confirmation, the employee becomes eligible for the benefits in section 6 that are limited to confirmed employees.

## 6. Benefits During Probation

### 6.1 Leave During Probation

Probationers earn annual leave month by month and may take leave they have already earned, as set out in the Leave Policy (BM-HR-POL-001), section 4.2. Sick leave and hospitalisation leave are available in full from the first day.

### 6.2 Medical Benefits During Probation

Probationers are covered for outpatient treatment at panel clinics for themselves only, and by the group hospitalisation insurance from their first day. Cover for family members, and dental and optical benefits, start on confirmation. See the Medical Claims and Benefits Policy (BM-HR-POL-002), sections 2 and 4.2.

### 6.3 Overtime, Claims and Work From Home

Probationers in NE grades are eligible for overtime under the Overtime Policy, and all probationers may claim business expenses in the same way as other employees. Probationers are not eligible for work-from-home arrangements (see the Work From Home Policy, section 2).

## 7. Notice Periods

### 7.1 Notice During Probation

Either the employee or the Company may end employment during probation by giving written notice:

| Grade | Notice during probation |
|---|---|
| NE1 to NE4 | 2 weeks |
| E1 to SM | 1 month |

> **Amendment (version 1.3, 1 February 2025):** The notice period during probation for E1 to SM was changed from 2 weeks to 1 month.

### 7.2 Notice After Confirmation

After confirmation, the notice period is:

| Grade | Notice after confirmation |
|---|---|
| NE1 to NE4 | 1 month |
| E1 to E3 | 2 months |
| M1, M2 and SM | 3 months |

These notice periods apply unless the employee's letter of appointment states a different period.

### 7.3 Payment Instead of Notice

Either party may pay salary instead of notice, for the whole notice period or the part not served. With HOD approval, untaken annual leave may be offset against the notice period (see the Leave Policy, section 4.4).

## 8. Ending Employment During Probation

8.1 The Company may end the employment of a probationer whose performance or conduct remains unsatisfactory after the reviews in section 3, or after an extension under section 4.

8.2 The decision must be supported by the documented reviews and approved by the HOD and the Head of Human Resources. The employee receives the reasons in writing.

8.3 Serious misconduct during probation is handled under the Code of Conduct, section 9, and may lead to dismissal without the review process.
