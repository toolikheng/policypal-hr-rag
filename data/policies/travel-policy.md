## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This policy covers business travel by employees of Binaan Maju Sdn Bhd ("the Company") that is outstation, meaning more than 100 km from the employee's base office or site, or overseas.

1.2 Local business trips of 100 km or less are covered by the Expense Claims Policy (BM-HR-POL-004).

## 2. Travel Approval

2.1 All outstation and overseas travel needs an approved Travel Request in MyHR before anything is booked.

2.2 Outstation travel is approved by the Head of Department (HOD). Overseas travel is approved by the HOD and by the Director of the division.

2.3 Overseas Travel Requests must be submitted at least 14 days before departure, except for urgent client or project matters approved by the Director.

2.4 Flights and hotels are booked through the Company's appointed travel agent where possible. Employees who book for themselves must choose the lowest reasonable fare.

## 3. Transport

### 3.1 Air Travel

3.1.1 All employees travel in economy class. Senior Management may travel in business class on flights with a scheduled flying time of more than six hours.

3.1.2 Flights are the normal mode of travel between Peninsular Malaysia and Sabah or Sarawak.

3.1.3 Excess baggage is reimbursed only for Company equipment or documents.

### 3.2 Using Your Own Vehicle for Outstation Travel

3.2.1 Employees may drive their own vehicle to outstation destinations within Peninsular Malaysia with HOD approval. Mileage is paid at the rates in the Expense Claims Policy, section 3.1, together with tolls.

3.2.2 For a one-way distance of more than 350 km, the total mileage claim for the trip is capped at the cost of a return economy flight on the same route, unless the HOD approves driving for a work reason, for example to carry site equipment.

### 3.3 Rail and Bus

Intercity train and express bus fares are reimbursed at cost. Employees may choose business or premium class on trains for journeys of more than three hours.

## 4. Accommodation

4.1 Hotel room limits per night, including service charge and tax:

| Grade | Kuala Lumpur, Penang and Johor Bahru | Other towns in Peninsular Malaysia | Sabah and Sarawak |
|---|---|---|---|
| NE1 to NE4 | RM180 | RM150 | RM200 |
| E1 to E3 | RM250 | RM200 | RM280 |
| M1 and M2 | RM350 | RM280 | RM380 |
| SM | Reasonable actual cost | Reasonable actual cost | Reasonable actual cost |

4.2 If no hotel within the limit is available, for example during a major event, the HOD may approve up to 20% above the limit. The reason must be stated in the claim.

4.3 Employees who stay with friends or relatives instead of at a hotel receive a lodging allowance of RM60 per night. No receipt is needed.

4.4 Employees on the same assignment should share transport where practical. Room sharing is not required.

4.5 For stays of more than 14 nights at one location, HR may arrange a serviced apartment instead of a hotel room.

## 5. Outstation Allowances

### 5.1 Outstation Meal Allowance

5.1.1 A daily meal allowance is paid for each day of outstation travel, instead of claiming individual meal receipts:

| Grade | Full day | Half day |
|---|---|---|
| NE1 to NE4 | RM50 | RM25 |
| E1 to E3 | RM70 | RM35 |
| M1 and M2 | RM90 | RM45 |
| SM | RM110 | RM55 |

5.1.2 The half-day rate applies on the day of departure if the employee leaves after 1:00 p.m., and on the day of return if the employee arrives back before 1:00 p.m.

5.1.3 Where meals are provided free, for example by a client or at a conference, the allowance is reduced by one third for each main meal provided.

### 5.2 Remote Site Allowance

Employees assigned to project sites in the interior of Sabah or Sarawak that can only be reached by four-wheel-drive vehicle, boat or light aircraft receive an additional RM30 per day.

## 6. Overseas Travel

### 6.1 Overseas Daily Allowance

6.1.1 Instead of the outstation meal allowance, overseas travellers receive a daily allowance in US dollars to cover meals, local transport and incidental expenses:

| Destination | Daily allowance |
|---|---|
| ASEAN countries | USD60 |
| Other countries in Asia | USD80 |
| Europe, North America, Australia and New Zealand | USD110 |
| All other destinations | USD80 |

6.1.2 The allowance is converted to Ringgit at the exchange rate on the date of departure.

### 6.2 Travel Insurance

All overseas business travel is covered by the Company's group travel insurance. HR must be told the travel dates at least three working days before departure so that cover can be confirmed.

### 6.3 Cash Advance

6.3.1 Employees may apply for a cash advance of up to 70% of the estimated trip cost at least seven days before departure.

6.3.2 The advance must be settled with a travel claim within 14 days of return. Advances not settled after 30 days are deducted from salary.

### 6.4 Passports and Visas

Visa fees for business travel are reimbursed. Passport renewal fees are not reimbursed.

## 7. Claims After Travel

7.1 Travel claims are submitted in eClaim within 14 days of returning, with the approved Travel Request, boarding passes and hotel invoices.

7.2 Late claims follow the deadlines in the Expense Claims Policy, section 8.
