## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out when employees of Binaan Maju Sdn Bhd ("the Company") may work from home (WFH), how arrangements are approved, and the rules that apply while working from home.

## 2. Eligibility

2.1 The following employees may apply for a regular WFH arrangement:

(a) confirmed office-based employees in grades E1 to SM; and

(b) confirmed office-based employees in NE grades whose work can be done remotely, with HOD approval.

2.2 Site-based employees are not eligible for regular WFH, because their work must be done at the project site.

2.3 Employees on probation are not eligible, so that they can learn the role in person.

2.4 Ad hoc WFH for a single day, for example while waiting for a home repair, may be approved by the immediate superior for any office-based employee, up to two days per month. Ad hoc WFH does not need an arrangement under section 4.

## 3. WFH Arrangements

3.1 An employee with an approved arrangement may work from home up to two days per week. The WFH days are agreed with the HOD in advance and do not change from week to week without approval.

3.2 A whole team may not work from home on the same day, so that every department has staff in the office on every working day.

3.3 While working from home, employees must be reachable by phone and the Company's messaging tools during core hours of 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. They must attend in-person meetings in the office when asked with at least one working day's notice.

## 4. Applying for WFH

4.1 Employees apply using the "Flexible Work Request" form in MyHR, stating the proposed WFH days and confirming that their home workspace meets section 7.

4.2 The HOD approves or rejects the request within 10 working days. Approved arrangements are reviewed every six months.

## 5. Equipment and Allowance

### 5.1 Company Equipment

The Company provides a laptop and, on request, a headset. Office furniture, monitors and printers for home use are not provided. Company equipment must be returned when the employee leaves the Company.

### 5.2 Internet and Utilities Allowance

Employees with an approved regular WFH arrangement receive a fixed allowance of RM50 per month, paid with their salary, toward home internet and electricity costs. No separate claims for internet or utility bills may be made. The allowance is not paid for ad hoc WFH under section 2.4.

> **Amendment (version 1.1, 1 October 2024):** The allowance was increased from RM30 to RM50 per month.

## 6. Working Hours and Overtime

6.1 Normal working hours and attendance rules apply while working from home. Employees log in on the MyHR app at the start and end of each WFH day.

6.2 NE employees working from home may only work overtime with prior approval under the Overtime Policy (BM-HR-POL-003), section 4.

## 7. Health and Safety at Home

7.1 Employees must have a safe, quiet workspace with a suitable chair and table, and must complete the home workspace self-checklist in MyHR once a year.

7.2 Injuries that happen while working from home during working hours must be reported to HR within 24 hours, in the same way as workplace incidents.

## 8. Information Security

8.1 Employees must connect to the Company network through the VPN when working from home. Public Wi-Fi must not be used without the VPN.

8.2 Confidential documents must not be printed at home. Screens must be locked when unattended, and family members must not use Company devices.

## 9. Ending or Suspending a WFH Arrangement

9.1 The Company may end or suspend a WFH arrangement with one week's written notice, for example because of operational needs, or if performance or availability falls below expectations.

9.2 An employee may end their own arrangement at any time by informing their HOD.
