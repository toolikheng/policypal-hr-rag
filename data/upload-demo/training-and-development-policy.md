## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Binaan Maju Sdn Bhd ("the Company") supports the learning and development of its employees through in-house training, sponsored external courses and professional certifications.

## 2. Eligibility

2.1 All permanent employees, including those on probation, may attend in-house training.

2.2 Sponsorship for external courses (section 5) and support for professional certifications (section 6) are available to confirmed employees only.

## 3. Training Days

3.1 Every employee should complete at least five days (40 hours) of training each calendar year. Training hours are recorded in MyHR under "Learning Record".

3.2 Approved training attended during working hours counts as working time.

3.3 Training held on a rest day at the Company's request is compensated: NE employees under the Overtime Policy, and executives with time off in lieu.

## 4. Training Needs Analysis

4.1 Every January, each employee and their immediate superior agree a training plan for the year, based on the skills the role needs and the employee's development goals.

4.2 HR combines the plans into an annual training calendar of in-house programmes, published in MyHR by the end of February.

## 5. External Course Sponsorship

5.1 The Company sponsors external courses up to the following amounts per employee per calendar year:

| Grade | Annual sponsorship limit |
|---|---|
| NE1 to NE4 | RM1,500 |
| E1 to E3 | RM3,000 |
| M1 and M2 | RM5,000 |
| SM | RM8,000 |

5.2 Employees apply at least 30 days before the course starts, using the Training Request form in MyHR. The HOD approves requests within the limit. Requests above the limit need Director approval.

5.3 Where possible the Company pays the course provider directly. Otherwise, the employee is reimbursed through eClaim after completing the course, on submitting the receipt and the certificate of attendance.

5.4 Travel to attend an external course follows the Travel Policy.

## 6. Professional Certifications

6.1 Examination fees for professional certifications approved by the HOD are reimbursed once the employee passes the examination. Fees for failed attempts are not reimbursed.

6.2 Employees receive one day of paid examination leave for each paper they sit, on the day of the examination, up to five days per calendar year.

## 7. Training Bond

7.1 An employee whose sponsorship for a single programme (an external course or certification) costs more than RM5,000 in total must sign a training bond before the programme starts.

7.2 The bond period is 12 months of service for every RM5,000 of sponsorship or part of RM5,000, up to a maximum of 36 months.

7.3 If the employee resigns, or is dismissed for misconduct, during the bond period, they must repay the sponsorship in proportion to the months of the bond not yet served.

7.4 Example: a programme costing RM12,000 has a bond period of 36 months. An employee who resigns after 12 months repays 24/36 of RM12,000, which is RM8,000.

## 8. Mandatory Safety Training for Site Employees

8.1 Before starting work at any project site, site-based employees must complete the Company's two-day site safety induction and hold a valid construction safety induction card.

8.2 A one-day safety refresher must be completed every two years. Employees whose refresher has lapsed may not enter a project site until it is completed.

## 9. Sharing What You Learn

Employees who attend external training of more than three days must share the key learnings with their team within one month, as a short presentation or a written summary.
