Welcome to the HR corner

This page stands in for a company intranet. The Ask HR policies button in the bottom-right corner is the PolicyPal widget: one script tag added to this page.

Binaan Maju Sdn Bhd and all its policies are fictional sample data, not real company policies and not legal advice.

To add the widget to a page on another site, include <script src="http://localhost:8000/widget/policypal-widget.js" defer></script> and add that site's origin to CORS_ORIGINS in .env.
